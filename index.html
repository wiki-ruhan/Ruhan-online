Engineering Safe & Sustainable Mobility

I am Rownak Shahriar Ruhan (formal name: A.A.M. Rownak Shahriar Ruhan), a Mechanical Engineering researcher at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) and the Founder & CEO of TriChokro Automotives. Founded in 2024 alongside fellow BUET engineers, TriChokro addresses the structural, braking, and aerodynamic vulnerabilities of conventional three-wheeled electric vehicles (Easy Bikes) across Bangladesh.

Academically advised and inspired by BUET Professor Dr. Md. Ehsan, our R&D integrates finite element structural analysis (SolidWorks), thermal brake fade and regenerative braking simulation (Ansys), and Kaizen manufacturing principles. In the open-knowledge movement, I serve as an Executive Member of Wikimedia Bangladesh (operating globally under the unified handle User:Ruhan), Chief Editor of Prohoranto, and Founder of Ronograph.

Flagship Passenger EV 1.8L – 3.0L BDT Lineup

TRC-Pathfinder (4 Commercial Trims)

Engineered to resolve 8 critical safety defects of traditional Easy Bikes. Features a reinforced truss chassis, triple hydraulic disc braking system, MacPherson strut front suspension, and an origami-inspired aerodynamic retractable hood.

  • • Trims: Starter (1.8L BDT), Weather-Sealed Pro IP69 (2.0L BDT), Extended Range 150km (2.5L BDT), Heavy Duty (3.0L BDT)
  • • Performance: 40 km/h max speed, 5 passengers, 6-hour charge cycle
UIHP 2025 Champion Emergency Response EV

Project Trambulance

A compact, zero-emission three-wheeled electric ambulance built to navigate congested urban lanes in Dhaka where conventional ambulances cannot reach in time.

  • • Capacity: 1 stretcher patient + 3 medical caregivers + life-support payload
  • • Validation: Aerodynamic hood and chassis validated via Ansys CFD and SolidWorks

Sister Concerns & Mobility Services

TribOriGen

Circular material science initiative recycling end-of-life waste tires into high-durability Ground Tire Rubber (GTR) elastomers for industrial and automotive applications.

TriMateria

Developing eco-efficient bio-composites to replace heavy, non-recyclable synthetic body panels in next-generation lightweight electric vehicles.

Road Safety Consultancy & Shuttle

Urban transport policy consultancy advocating engineering upgrades over blanket auto-rickshaw bans, alongside the 10-Taka BUET–TSC electric campus shuttle and TriChokro Cargo.
